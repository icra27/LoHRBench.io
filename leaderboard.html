How the rankings are calculated

These rankings are computed from the per-task results in Table II. Each method is scored on all tasks for which it has reported results.

The score is the unweighted mean across the reported tasks. Methods are ranked within their family by mean success rate, with mean progress as the tie-breaker. Selecting progress reverses the priority. Rankings use unrounded values; displayed scores are rounded.

PDDLStream, ACT, DP, OpenVLA-oft, and π₀ (FAST) are averaged over eight tasks. TiPToP and Agentic are averaged over six, as their active exploration results are not reported. Methods therefore have different task coverage; the count is shown beside each score. Missing results are never treated as zero. VLA and other-policy filters show ranks within the selected subtype.
