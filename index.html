The benchmark

Abstract

Long-horizon manipulation is challenging: as horizons grow, errors compound and small deviations cascade. Modular planning systems and end-to-end policies approach this problem differently, yet are rarely evaluated under the same tasks and protocol. We introduce LOHR-BENCH (Long-Horizon Reasoning Benchmark), a dual-level, method-agnostic arena of 20 tasks across four suites: Tool-use, Active Exploration, Clutter, and Extended-long-horizon. It supports both modular TAMP methods and end-to-end policies within the same environment. Tasks are specified in Planning Domain Definition Language (PDDL) and grounded in simulators, enabling strategy-independent evaluation through goal predicates. The benchmark provides (i) a data pipeline with synchronized rollouts that align language, symbolic plans, and low-level trajectories, (ii) a concise Gym-style interface supporting high-level, low-level and dual-level policies, and (iii) unified metrics for progress, success, decision time, and execution time. Together, these components offer a focused, reproducible testbed for comparing modular and end-to-end approaches and for developing hybrid methods that combine structured planning with learned policies.

Explore the tasks

20 manipulation tasks, organized by the challenge they introduce.

Showing all 20 tasks across 4 suites

01

Tool-use 5 tasks

Use tools and containers to move, transfer, and store objects.

Place fruit with a container

fruit_placement

Move four strawberries to the target position. A bowl is available to help transport them.

Repackage cubes

repackage

Put all cubes into the bowl and stack the bowl on the plate.

Transfer between containers

container_transfer

Transfer items from one container into another and place the destination container on a plate.

Store food in a locker

food_storage_simple

Store a bowl containing a strawberry inside a locker.

Push items with a T-tool

push_T_items

Move the cubes and strawberries to the target location. Several T-shaped blocks of different sizes are available to help move them.

02

Active exploration 5 tasks

Open cabinets and drawers to find and retrieve hidden objects.

Find and stack cubes

stack_active

Retrieve cubes from a closed drawer and stack them together, starting with the red cube.

Retrieve objects from drawers

pick_active

Retrieve the can, screwdriver, and cup from a two-layer drawer and place them at their target locations.

Search cluttered drawers

drawer_search_hard

Retrieve the can, screwdriver, and cup from a cluttered two-drawer cabinet and place them at their target locations.

Stack cubes from a cabinet

stack_cube_cabinet

Retrieve cubes from a closed two-level cabinet and stack them.

Retrieve food from a cabinet

food_retrieval

Retrieve two strawberries and two apples from the cabinet. Place the strawberries in the bowl and the apples on the plate. Both containers are on the table.

03

Clutter 5 tasks

Manipulate objects among obstacles and tightly packed surroundings.

Stack cubes in clutter

stack_clutter

Stack three cubes together in a compact environment with surrounding obstacles.

Pack cubes from clutter

clutter_packing

Pick three cubes from a cluttered tabletop and place them into a bowl.

Retrieve from tall clutter

tall_clutter

Retrieve a cube surrounded by taller cubes and place it into a bowl.

Extract a stacked cuboid

clutter_draw

Extract the red cuboid from beneath the middle column of the three-column stack.

Push a cube through clutter

clutter_push_cube

Push a cube to a target location through clutter, moving obstacles that block the path.

04

Extended long-horizon 5 tasks

Complete long sequences of stacking, sorting, and rearrangement.

Stack 10 cubes

stack_cube_10

Stack 10 cubes together, starting with the red cube.

Sort and store

sort_and_store

Sort tabletop objects into two boxes: toys and balls in one box, tools in the other.

Reverse a stack

reverse_stack

Reverse a stack of 10 cubes.

Build a cube pyramid

stack_cube_pyramid_simple

Build a 10-cube pyramid with four layers containing 4, 3, 2, and 1 cubes, respectively.

Cube Tower of Hanoi

stack_cube_hanoi

Solve a Tower-of-Hanoi-style rearrangement with colored cubes using pick-and-place under Hanoi constraints.
